Click the map to read a temperature.

H₀, Ωm, Ωb, ΩΛ, w, TCMB and Neff come from the calculator. The peak positions use the real sound horizon and angular diameter distance. The peak heights come from a toy model tuned by eye to Planck. Good for intuition, teaching and slides. For papers, use CAMB or CLASS.

The points are the power spectrum measured from this one map, and the error bars are cosmic variance. You only get one sky. You can't ask for a second one, so the scatter at low ℓ stays.
